How it works

  1. Describe your experience. Talk with Vera about your professional and lived background.
  2. Name a target course. Vera helps map your skills to a specific course and the claims you can make.
  3. Add artifacts. Upload certificates, transcripts, or work products and link each to a claim.
  4. Submit for assessment. When your portfolio is strong enough, send it to faculty for a decision.
